**What it shows**

- **For 1,720, both China and the rest of world need only 65–80% of what they normally book from here**, whichever way the Unknown is counted. Both are booking far above that today.
- **Most of the season's buying is still ahead of us in Nov–Feb.** About 55–85% of what is still needed normally comes before the end of February. So the next three months decide the number.
- **China:** at the current pace it reaches 25 Mt between mid-December and early January. After that its weekly sales should drop close to zero if the 25 Mt cap holds.
- **Rest of world:** after February the requirement is light (~95–180 kt/week). The risk to 1,720 being too low is a sharp rest-of-world slowdown from December, not the spring.

**Bottom line:** on a normal pace from here, exports land around 1,840–1,890 (depending on how the Unknown is counted). USDA's 1,720 needs buying to fall to 65–80% of normal for the rest of the season.
