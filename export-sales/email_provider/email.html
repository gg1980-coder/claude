Why we think the risk is to the upside

  • The current pace is above both numbers. The rest of the world is booking ~20% above what 1,700 needs and ~15% above what 1,720 needs.
  • The strength is broad. Four of seven regions are ahead of the 1,720 pace and one is on pace (table below). Only Mexico and East Asia are weak.
  • The alternative explanation is China. If the rest of the world books normally, 1,700 implies China stops at ~21.8 Mt (only ~2.5 Mt more than today's 19.3 Mt), against a 25 Mt commitment.
  • Brazil alone doesn't close the gap. Even with a record 33.5 Mt of Brazilian exports to non-China buyers, our model gives ~1,728.
  • Probability: on our model (10-season backtest, error ±3 Mt), there is a ~71% chance of finishing above 1,700 and ~64% above 1,720. 1,700 is possible, but it is the low end.
