**Subject: US soybean exports: China and rest of world vs USDA 1,720, what is needed by end-February**

Hi all,

Quick update on where US soybean sales stand against USDA's 1,720 mln bu (46.8 Mt), with China at 25 Mt. Data: USDA export sales through week 10 (w/e 5 Nov). I show it both ways for the "Unknown" destinations (5.4 Mt): 60% counted as China (my approach) and all counted as rest of world (the approach some analysts use).

**What is needed from now to end-February (week 26, w/e 25 Feb) to stay on pace for 1,720**

| | Booked so far (Mt) | Still to book, full season (Mt) | Needed by end-Feb (Mt) | Needed per week (kt) | Normal pace, same weeks (kt/wk, 10-yr) | Last 4 weeks (kt/wk) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **China**, Unknown 60% to China | 19.9 | 5.1 | 4.5 | **~280** | ~420 | **~1,300** |
| **China**, named only | 16.6 | 8.4 | 6.8 | **~425** | ~550 | **~1,340** |
| **Rest of world**, 40% of Unknown | 9.4 | 12.4 | 7.3 | **~455** | ~585 | **~730** |
| **Rest of world**, incl. all Unknown | 14.8 | 7.1 | 4.2 | **~265** | ~370 | **~665** |

"Needed by end-Feb" = the share of the remaining season that is normally booked by week 26. "Normal" = 10-season average (2016/17–2025/26), for both China and rest of world.

**What it shows**

- **China is well ahead either way.** It needs ~280–425 kt/week to stay on pace and is booking ~1,300. At this speed China reaches 25 Mt in early-to-mid December. The key signal from here: if China sales drop sharply in December, the 25 Mt cap is holding; if they continue into January, there is upside to the 25 Mt assumption.
- **The rest of world is also ahead either way.** It needs ~265–455 kt/week, about 70–80% of its normal 10-year pace for these weeks, and is booking ~665–730.
- **Both approaches point the same way:** USDA's 1,720 needs both China and the rest of world to book less than normal from here. The only way to 1,720 or below is a sharp slowdown in rest-of-world buying from December, or China stopping well short of 25 Mt.

**What to watch each Thursday**

- China: weekly sales falling below ~300 kt by mid-December = cap holding.
- Rest of world: staying above ~455 kt/week (or ~265 incl. Unknown) keeps exports on track above 1,720. Weak spots today: Mexico, Japan, Korea, Indonesia, Germany.
