**Subject: US soybean exports: China and rest of world vs USDA 1,720, what is needed by end-February**

Hi all,

Quick update on where US soybean sales stand against USDA's 1,720 mln bu (46.8 Mt), with China at 25 Mt. Data: USDA export sales through week 11 (w/e 12 Nov). I show it both ways for the "Unknown" destinations (5.2 Mt): 60% counted as China (my approach) and all counted as rest of world (the approach some analysts use).

**This week:** net sales 1.50 Mt. Rest of world (named) +1.11 Mt, China +0.58 Mt, Unknown −0.19 Mt.

**What is needed from now to end-February (weeks 12–26, w/e 25 Feb) to stay on pace for 1,720**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **China**, Unknown 60% to China | 20.3 | 4.7 | 4.1 | **~270** | ~405 | **~870** |

"Needed by end-Feb" = the share of the remaining season that is normally booked by week 26. "Normal" = 10-season average (2016/17–2025/26), for both China and rest of world.

**What it shows**

- **China is well ahead either way.** It needs ~270–415 kt/week to stay on pace and is booking ~870–945. At this speed China reaches 25 Mt between mid-December and early January. The key signal from here: if China sales drop sharply then, the 25 Mt cap is holding; if they continue, there is upside to the 25 Mt assumption.
- **The rest of world is also ahead either way.** It needs ~235–430 kt/week, about 65–75% of its normal pace for these weeks, and is booking ~730–850.
- **Both approaches point the same way:** USDA's 1,720 needs both China and the rest of world to book less than normal from here. The only way to 1,720 or below is a sharp slowdown in rest-of-world buying from December, or China stopping well short of 25 Mt.

**What to watch each Thursday**

- China: weekly sales falling below ~300 kt by late December = cap holding.
- Rest of world: staying above ~430 kt/week (or ~235 incl. Unknown) keeps exports on track above 1,720. Weak spots today: Japan, Korea, Germany and the Netherlands. Mexico, Indonesia and Egypt picked up this week.
