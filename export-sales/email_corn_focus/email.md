**Subject: US corn exports: Mexico, Japan, Korea and Europe vs USDA 3,275, what is needed by end-February and end-August**

Hi all,

Where US corn sales stand for the four main buyers against USDA's 3,275 mln bu, on a 10-year pace. Data: USDA export sales through week 10 (w/e 5 Nov). USDA's number is converted to the weekly-sales basis (80.4 Mt).

**In one line:** to reach 3,275, every buyer has to book about **55% more than its 10-year normal** from here. Only Europe is clearly doing that today, and Mexico thanks to one very large week; Japan, Korea and the smaller buyers are not.

"Needed" shares USDA's total out in proportion to each buyer's usual 10-year bookings from this point, and follows the normal seasonal pattern. Unknown destinations are not assigned to a buyer; the history already reflects them being named later.

**What it shows**

- **USDA's 3,275 needs a record or near-record year from all the main buyers at once:** Japan at a record, Mexico and Korea at their second highest.
- **Mexico is the one buyer keeping up**, but mostly thanks to one 2.0 Mt week; the three weeks before ran at ~390 kt/week: above normal (~315), but below the ~490 USDA needs.
- **Japan is the clearest shortfall:** it needs ~380 kt/week to end-February and is booking ~195, below even its normal pace.
- **Korea and the smaller buyers are also behind.** Europe is ahead, but it is a small market for corn.

**What to watch each Thursday:** Mexico holding above ~490 kt/week and Japan recovering towards ~380. Without both, the case for a USDA cut keeps building.
