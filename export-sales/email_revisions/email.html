What history says about USDA

  • The pace pointed the right way for USDA's later revision in 7 of 10 seasons for corn, 8 of 10 for soybeans.
  • USDA moves in small steps (corn ~50, soybeans ~20 a month) and rarely changes exports in December.
  • January is the first report where a move becomes likely.
