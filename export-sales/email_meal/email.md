**Subject: US soybean meal exports: where we stand vs USDA and Dnext (sales to w/e 12 November)**

Hi all,

A short update on US soybean meal export sales, using USDA's weekly export sales (meal week 7, w/e 12 Nov) and Dnext's trade-flow forecast by destination.

**In one line:** sales are level with last year's record, which is consistent with Dnext's ~17.6 Mt. USDA's 22.7 mln short tons (20.6 Mt) needs ~12% growth on last year, and on the current pace looks ~15% too high.

**Where sales stand (week 7)**

| | This season | vs last season | vs 5-yr average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sold so far (shipped + unshipped) | 7.0 Mt | 0% | +17% |
| Shipped so far | 2.0 Mt | +6% | +25% |
| Latest week's sales | 198 kt | 358 kt same week last season | seasonal need: 431 kt |

**Full-year view (Mt)**

| | 2026/27 | vs 2025/26 (18.4) |
|---|---|---|
| USDA (Nov WASDE) | 20.6 | +12% |
| Dnext | 17.6 | −4% |
| Current sales pace (normal share of the year sold by now) | ~17.6 | −4% |

By this point of the season, buyers normally have 43% of the year booked. Sales so far are exactly 43% of Dnext's forecast, so the pace is in line with Dnext. USDA would need sales to run ~17% faster from here.

**What it shows**

- **The total is on Dnext's track, not USDA's.** Sales are flat on last year's record; USDA's number needs another ~3 Mt.
- **The country mix is uneven.** Europe, Vietnam and Korea booked early and heavily; Ecuador, Morocco, Indonesia and Central America have barely started. Dnext looks too low for the first group and too high for the second, but the two roughly offset.
- **Europe is the standout:** 642 kt sold vs 49 kt a year ago, already ~75% of Dnext's full-year forecast. Weekly sales are still ~30 kt, which points to genuinely higher demand rather than only buying early. I see Europe at ~1.3–2.0 Mt for the year vs Dnext's ~0.9 Mt.
- **The latest week was weak** (198 kt, half the seasonal need): Vietnam, the Philippines and Korea went quiet and Japan cancelled.

**Caveats**

- Meal is pushed by record US crush. If meal is priced to move, sales can pick up later, so a slow start is a weaker signal than for beans or corn.
- The "normal share sold by now" approach overstates destinations that booked unusually early (Europe, Vietnam, Korea).
- ~0.44 Mt of sales are still to "Unknown" destinations and will be named later, mostly in Asia.

**What to watch:** weekly sales back above ~430 kt (the seasonal need), Southeast Asian buyers returning, and whether Europe holds ~20–30 kt/week through December.
