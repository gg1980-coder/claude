**Subject: US soybean exports 2026/27 — desk number 1,800 mln bu vs USDA 1,720, and the milestones to track**

Team,

**Bottom line**

- **Our number: 1,800 mln bu**, vs USDA's 1,720 (Nov WASDE). We put the chance of finishing above USDA at **~76%**. This assumes China buys **exactly 25 Mt** from the US.
- With China fixed, the open question is **how much the rest of the world buys**. It is on pace today. The main risk is **Brazil** selling more to non-China buyers.
- **If we're right, US ending stocks are ~220 mln bu, not 300.** USDA has room to raise exports in the coming reports.

**How we got there (in short)**

- We used 10 seasons of USDA weekly export sales by destination. For each region, we added what it has already booked to what it normally books from now until August.
- China is set at 25 Mt (policy, not seasonality). Brazil's competition is measured from 10 seasons of US vs Brazil shipments to the same buyers.
- Backtested over 10 seasons, this method was within about ±3 Mt of the final number. The simple "share booked by now" multiplier was off by up to ±12 Mt.
