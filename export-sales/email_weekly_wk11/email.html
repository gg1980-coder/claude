Subject: US export sales: soybeans, corn and soybean meal, week ending 12 November

Hi all,

Quick summary of the latest USDA export sales (soybeans and corn week 11, soybean meal week 7, all w/e 12 Nov) against USDA's November forecasts.

In one line: soybeans are running ahead of USDA, corn and meal behind.

USDA forecast Sold so far Latest week Latest week vs seasonal need Cumulative vs pace USDA needs Risk to USDA number
Soybeans 1,720 mln bu 32.9 Mt 1.50 Mt +0.19 Mt +19% upside
Corn 3,275 mln bu 29.3 Mt 1.59 Mt −0.45 Mt −19% downside
Soybean meal 22.7 mln st 7.0 Mt 0.20 Mt −0.23 Mt −14% downside

Soybeans: ahead, and the mix is shifting from China to the rest of world

  • China slowing as it approaches its 25 Mt commitment (0.87 → 0.69 → 0.58 Mt over the last three weeks); rest of world had its best week of the season (1.11 Mt), led by Mexico, Turkey, Vietnam, Spain and Indonesia.
  • With China at 25 Mt, 1,720 needs the rest of world at only ~75% of its normal 10-year pace (~430 kt/week to end-February); it is booking ~850.
  • On a normal pace from here, exports land ~1,840–1,890. Weak spots: Japan, Korea, Germany.

Corn: behind, and slowing outside Mexico

  • Mexico bought 2.0 Mt and 0.9 Mt in the last two weeks; excluding Mexico, weekly sales fell to 0.66 Mt.
  • 3,275 needs every buyer at ~155% of its normal pace, including a record year for Japan; Japan is booking well under half of what is needed. Shipments are also behind (0.94 Mt vs ~1.5 Mt seasonal).
  • I expect USDA to cut, most likely starting in January and in steps, towards ~2,750–2,950.

Soybean meal: flat on last year's record, short of USDA's +12%

  • Sales are level with last year and +29% vs the 10-year average, but USDA needs ~12% growth on last year's record.
  • Week 7 was weak (0.20 Mt vs 0.43 needed): Vietnam, the Philippines and Korea went quiet and Japan cancelled. Mexico is −23% vs last year; Europe is the standout.
  • On pace, USDA looks ~6–15% too high. Caveat: meal is pushed by record US crush, so sales can pick up later if meal is priced to move.

What to watch next week

  • Soybeans: rest of world above ~430 kt/week; China sales tapering towards zero by late December (cap holding).
  • Corn: Mexico holding up, Japan recovering; sales excluding Mexico back above ~1 Mt/week.
  • Meal: Southeast Asian buyers returning.
